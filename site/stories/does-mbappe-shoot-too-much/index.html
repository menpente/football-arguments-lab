Does Mbappe shoot too much?

Is Mbappe taking an unusually large share of his team's shots for his role?

hook

Does Mbappe shoot too much?

Here is the claim as it travelled online: Does Mbappe shoot too much?. It sounds decisive. The data says otherwise — or at least, not yet.

refine

A sharper question

"Does Mbappe shoot too much?" is not testable as written. The workable version is: Is Mbappe taking an unusually large share of his team's shots for his role?

operationalize

What we actually measured

To answer that, we tracked shots, goals, conversion, xg, xg_per_shot, shots on target.

evidence

What the numbers show

Mbappe: 24 shots, 4 goals (16.67% conversion), 4.21 xG, 0.18 xG/shot. Raphinha: 10 shots, 5 goals (50.0% conversion), 4.1 xG, 0.41 xG/shot.

evidence

Volume vs. efficiency

Raw shot totals and conversion rate side by side.

evidence

Shot quality

xG per shot approximates how good the chances actually were.

complication

It's not that simple

Mbappe is scoring below their xG (-0.21), which looks like underperformance more than bad shot selection. Raphinha's +0.90 goals-vs-xG gap is hard to sustain over a full season — some regression is likely before a verdict on 'better finisher' should be trusted.

verdict

The strongest defensible verdict

The volume or pattern behind the claim can be demonstrated, but a judgement about quality or intent requires evidence beyond the headline number.
